ABOUT

A Night of Bon Odori

Bon Odori is a traditional Japanese summer dance. Participants form a large circle around a central tower (yagura) and dance the same simple steps together to live taiko drumming. This is a genuine, community-rooted festival, and you're warmly invited to join the circle and dance alongside local residents — though simply watching is welcome too.
