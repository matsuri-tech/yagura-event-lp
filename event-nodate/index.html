ABOUT

Carry the Mikoshi

A mikoshi is a portable shrine believed to carry a local deity, which residents hoist onto their shoulders and carry through the streets once a year, chanting together as they go — a lively, energetic centerpiece of Japanese summer festivals. Local carriers are opening a limited number of spots to our hotel guests this year. You'll wear a traditional happi coat and carry the mikoshi shoulder-to-shoulder with local residents. No experience is needed — our staff will guide you on the day.
